How to use this dashboard:

1. Ensure your Excel file has headers matching your standard format (e.g. Course Code, Course Title, Staff Incharge, Shift, Reg. Nol, Name).

2. Set your Category ID (where the courses will be built) and your Template Course shortname (the master shell containing your quiz).

3. Click Generate. Two files will automatically download:

  • Upload Courses: Upload this in your LMS > Courses > Upload courses.
  • Upload Users: Upload this in your LMS > Users > Upload users (Update existing users only).
