How to use this dashboard:

1. Ensure your file is a CSV (like end_spl.csv) or Excel file with headers (e.g. coursecode, coursetitle, staffname / staffid, shift, regdno / Reg. Nol).

2. Set your Category ID, Template Course shortname, and Date Suffix (e.g. 15102026).

3. Click Generate. Two files will automatically download:

  • Upload Courses: Upload this in your LMS > Courses > Upload courses.
  • Upload Users: Upload this in your LMS > Users > Upload users (Update existing users only).
